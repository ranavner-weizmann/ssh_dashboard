Flights are detected automatically from this date's merged drone telemetry: altitude_agl_m climbing to 2m marks takeoff (minus a 2-minute lead-in), dropping to and staying below 0.5m for 2 minutes marks landing. Add one or more sensor variables to see their vertical profiles, one chart per flight. Each instrument reading is tagged with the drone's altitude at that moment (nearest telemetry sample within 5s), then grouped into bins centred on the hover levels - with 20m bins, the 60m bin holds everything from 50 to 70m - and drawn as a mean line with a shaded ±1 std band; the tooltip also shows the median, quartiles and the number of instrument readings in the bin. "Hover only" keeps readings taken while the drone held a level (altitude steady within 2m over ±5s) and drops the climb between levels. With several variables on one chart, each is scaled to its own mean±std range (real units in the tooltip), same as the Plot tab.
After the profile the drone transits out (that leg is ignored) and then comes back in at constant altitude, pausing at hover points every 100 m, the last one over the launch area. The run is found from the telemetry alone: it starts at the flight's farthest point from the launch position (needs at least 150 m) and runs until the drone lands, keeping only samples at the run's own altitude. The same variables as above are plotted against horizontal distance from launch, in bins centred on the hover points (with 100 m bins, the 300 m bin holds 250-350 m) - the drone flies right-to-left on this chart. "Hover only" keeps readings taken while the drone held position (within 3 m over ±5 s) and drops the legs between hover points. The chart title gives the run's altitude; the status line lists the hover points found.
Pearson correlations between the ticked variables, using only readings taken during flights (from each flight's start, with its 2-minute ground lead-in, to its landing). The selection drives all three grids. All variables correlates the variables' mean values per time step (30 s by default, since the MA200 reports every 30 s and the POM every ~10 s) within the chosen flights. Ground vs height treats each flight as one sample: a variable's mean in the 0 m bin (the drone on the ground and at the 0 m hover) is correlated across flights with the mean at each hover level (20 m bins, hovering readings only) - either of the same variable, or of one target variable, for example how ground temperature, humidity and PM relate to ozone at each height. Station vs distance does the same along the linear route, between the hover point over the launch area (0 m) and the hover points every 100 m out to the far point. A cell is blank when fewer than 2 pairs exist; hover a cell for its number of pairs. Correlations across flights only mean much with several flights - pick "all downloaded days" to pool every merged day under the downloads folder.
